2. Nature and Scope of Services

Red Desert Check-In provides non-medical, in-person check-ins, home safety assessments, observation, companionship or presence within the agreed scope, light practical help, property observations, and written updates or findings after visits. Additional tasks may be agreed upon in advance and included in the Customer Agreement.

Seasonal Home Watch means scheduled, in-person visual checks of an unoccupied home on the agreed visit schedule, with a written or photo report after each visit, plus agreed tasks such as adjusting lights or blinds, moving trash bins, collecting packages, and changing outdoor decor. Home watch is not continuous security monitoring, alarm response, or property management, and Red Desert Check-In does not perform repairs. When a problem is found, Red Desert Check-In will notify the client or the client's designated contact, and repairs are arranged by the client or by a provider the client authorizes. Home watch plans have a three-month minimum and are billed monthly until canceled.

Test & Connect is a membership of scheduled 30-minute visits, offered monthly, every two weeks, or weekly, that combines a test of the customer's existing medical alert with non-medical companionship and a general well-being check. Testing follows each medical alert provider's procedures and is limited to pressing or triggering the customer's equipment, confirming that a test signal was received, and reviewing emergency contact information. Red Desert Check-In does not directly access, change, or manage monitoring company accounts; account changes are requested by the customer or an authorized person through the provider's approved process, with Red Desert Check-In helping only as asked. A successful test confirms the equipment worked at the time of the visit and is not a guarantee of future performance, signal coverage, or emergency response. Red Desert Check-In does not sell, install, or repair medical alert equipment. Companionship activities are chosen by the customer. Walks are side by side without hands-on physical support. Red Desert Check-In does not drive clients. Any costs for activities are paid by the customer. Customers without a medical alert may receive the same visit without the test.

Red Desert Check-In does not provide medical care, home health services, medication management, emergency response, security monitoring, hands-on personal care, transportation, ongoing or full-time caregiving, routine childcare or babysitting, full housekeeping, repairs or maintenance, full IT support, or overnight/live-in house sitting unless a specific non-conflicting service is expressly agreed in writing.
