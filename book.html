Step 2

What happens on the booking form?

Once you choose a visit option, the booking form collects the details I need to show up prepared. This keeps payment connected to the correct booking type.

You will still be able to tell me exactly what kind of check-in this is for before the visit.

Questions to include in your booking form

  • What type of check-in is this?
  • Are you interested in any add-ons? I’ll confirm separately if there is an added cost.
  • Who or what am I checking on?
  • What is the visit address?
  • Who should receive the update?
  • Anything I should know before I arrive?

Optional add-ons

Add-ons can be requested during booking.

Koalender does not charge add-ons automatically. If you request one, I’ll confirm it separately and let you know if there is an added cost before the visit.

Meal Train Setup

I create a simple meal train link you can send to family, friends, neighbors, or coworkers.

Video Walkthrough

Add a short video walkthrough when you want a clearer visual update from the visit.

Extra Time

Add 30 minutes when the situation needs more time, support, or conversation.

Important: Red Desert Check-In is not emergency response, nursing care, medication management, full-time caregiving, full housekeeping, or a replacement for 911. Updates are sent to one designated contact after the visit.
