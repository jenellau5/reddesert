What it is

A weekly visit that does two jobs.

Test & Connect is a non-medical senior support service that combines routine medical alert testing with real, personal connection. Every week, I make sure the help button works and the emergency contacts are current. Then we spend the rest of the visit doing something your loved one enjoys.

Choose your connection

Test & Walk

Enjoy a comfortable walk, fresh air, and friendly conversation.

We walk side by side at their pace. I do not provide hands-on support.

Test & Talk

Sit down for coffee, conversation, storytelling, or simply having someone who listens.

Test & Lunch

Enjoy lunch together at home, or meet at a favorite local restaurant.

Food and other costs are not included. I do not drive clients, so restaurant lunches mean meeting there.

Test & Play

Work on puzzles, play cards, enjoy board games, read together, or another favorite activity.

Change the activity any week. No medical alert? Same visit and price, we just skip the test, and I can help you compare options on the Community Board.

Every visit includes

The check, then the connection.

  • Test the medical alert base unit and wearable button, as applicable.
  • Confirm successful test signals with the monitoring center.
  • Review emergency contact information and spot outdated details.
  • Help submit requested account updates to the medical alert provider, using the provider's approved process.
  • Notice equipment concerns and help notify the right provider.
  • Friendly companionship and the activity they chose.
  • A brief visit update to authorized family members.

Works with different providers

Test & Connect is available for customers with different medical alert providers, following each provider's testing procedures.

Red Desert Check-In is independent. I do not directly access or change monitoring company accounts, sell equipment, or take referral fees. Account changes are always made by you or your loved one through the provider.
