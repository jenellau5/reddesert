Check-in types

Common reasons people reach out

One service, used in different real-life situations. If yours does not fit neatly, a custom check-in is always an option.

Aging parent or loved one

For the moments when you are far away and something feels off. A check-in visit means a real person goes over, makes sure they are okay, and reports back to you directly.

  • Parent or grandparent living alone
  • Someone in recovery or managing health changes
  • A friend you have not been able to reach
  • Someone who says they are fine, but you are not sure

Hospital support

New

If someone you love has been taken to the hospital and family is still on the way, a check-in means someone calm and caring can go sit with them, let them know they are not alone, and give you updates until you arrive.

This is not medical care. It is a human presence — someone to sit nearby, speak calmly, and communicate with you so no one is waiting alone in an unfamiliar place.

  • Sitting with someone until family arrives
  • Providing calm company and reassurance
  • Sending regular updates to a designated contact
  • Staying as long as needed (discussed ahead of time)

Home & property

When you are traveling, at work, or simply cannot get there, a property check-in means a set of reliable eyes on what you care about.

  • Traveling or extended time away
  • Second homes and snowbird properties
  • Mail, packages, lights, doors, obvious concerns
  • Peace-of-mind walkthrough after a storm or worry

Pets

Quick visits when your pet needs attention and you cannot get there. Not full-time pet sitting — just a reliable check when it matters.

  • Food and water check
  • Letting them out or a few minutes of attention
  • Backup between regular pet care visits
  • Quick welfare check when something feels off

Rental or vacant home

For vacation rentals, Airbnbs, and vacant properties that need a set of eyes between visits or after a guest stay.

  • Condition check after a guest checkout
  • Cleaning or maintenance verification
  • Vacant property check for security or weather concerns
  • Quick walkthrough with photos sent to you

Business or property check

For business owners who need someone local to go look at something without making a full trip.

  • Business spot check after hours or while you are away
  • Construction or contractor follow-up
  • Complaint or concern follow-up
  • Quick exterior or condition walkthrough

Custom situation

For the thing that does not fit a category. If something feels off and you need a calm, reliable person to go look — that qualifies.

  • Something feels off and you want it checked
  • A unique one-time request
  • A specific person, place, or concern
  • "I just need someone local to go look"

Add-ons & special requests

Some situations need a little more. If your visit involves any of the following, just mention it ahead of time and we will work it out together.

  • Extra time on-site
  • Video walkthroughs for a more visual update
  • More detailed written reports
  • Waiting with someone until family or help arrives
  • Multiple stops in one visit
  • Recurring check-ins on a regular schedule
  • Any other special situation or custom need

Whenever possible, any add-ons or extra time are discussed and agreed on before the visit so there are no surprises.
