Check-in types

Common reasons people reach out

One clear service, used in different real-life situations. If your need does not fit neatly into one category, book a custom check-in.

Check-In on Someone

For the moments when you cannot be there yourself.

Examples

  • Aging parent living alone
  • College student away from home
  • Friend you have not heard from
  • Someone in recovery
  • New or overwhelmed mom

Support During a Hard Season

For times of illness, overwhelm, loss, or recovery.

Examples

  • After surgery or illness
  • During burnout or emotional overwhelm
  • After a major life change
  • When someone says they are fine, but you are not sure

Home & Property Check-In

When you need someone local to go look around.

Examples

  • Traveling or on vacation
  • Second homes and snowbird properties
  • Mail, trash, doors, lights, or obvious issues
  • Quick peace-of-mind walkthrough
  • Walkthrough of a potential home before or during the buying process
  • Checking on new build progress while you are out of town

Rental & Business Check-In

For rental properties, business locations, and on-site follow-up when you cannot be there yourself.

Examples

  • Check on a rental property between guests or visits
  • Walk through a vacation rental or Airbnb
  • Confirm a cleaner, vendor, or maintenance visit
  • Check on your business while you are out of town
  • See how things look on-site and report back
  • Follow up on a complaint or concern
  • Check progress on a repair, project, or contractor visit
  • Quick walkthrough after an issue or unexpected concern

Hospital Check-In

For the moments when someone has been taken to the hospital and you are not nearby yet.

Examples

  • A loved one was taken to the hospital and you are still on the way
  • You want someone local to stop by and be there until family arrives
  • You need a calm in-person visit and a clear update
  • You want someone to sit with them for a short time so they are not alone

Custom Check-In

For the thing that does not fit neatly in a box.

Examples

  • Something feels off and you want it checked
  • A unique one-time request
  • A specific person, place, item, or area
  • I just need someone local to go look

What a visit may include

Practical help and a clear update

  • In-person check-in visit
  • Basic walkthrough and eyes on the situation
  • Conversation and connection when appropriate
  • Small help during the visit, like trash, packages, or light reset
  • Photos when useful and appropriate
  • Clear update sent to one designated contact

Some visits may also include extra time, a video walkthrough, waiting with someone for a short period, or other special requests depending on the situation.

Clear boundaries

What this is not

  • Not emergency response
  • Not nursing care
  • Not medication management
  • Not full-time caregiving
  • Not full housekeeping
  • Not a replacement for 911

Sometimes peace of mind starts with someone simply showing up.

A check-in is not just a quick stop. It is a way to get real information, catch small things before they become bigger problems, and feel less in the dark when you cannot see for yourself.
