Frequently asked questions

Common questions answered.

What exactly is Red Desert Check-In?

Red Desert Check-In provides non-medical, in-person support for people, homes, rentals, businesses, hospital situations, and practical concerns when you need someone local to go check, be present, and send a clear update afterward.

How much does a visit cost?

Most visits are $50 for approximately 45–60 minutes. Some visits may include extra time, add-ons, or custom support when arranged in advance.

What do I receive after a visit?

You receive a written update after the visit, and photos when appropriate and authorized. The goal is to help you understand what was seen, how things seemed, and whether anything needs follow-up.

Do you provide medical care?

No. Red Desert Check-In is non-medical support only. This is not nursing care, medication management, medical decision-making, or emergency response.

Do you provide transportation?

No. Appointment support means meeting someone there, being present, and helping them feel supported before, during, or after the appointment. Transportation is not included.

Can visits be longer than an hour?

Sometimes. Most visits are approximately 45–60 minutes, but some caregiver relief visits and new parent support visits may be available up to 3 hours when arranged in advance. Extended hospital sitting may also be available in longer blocks by advance agreement.

Can you help with aging parents or family caregivers?

Yes. Red Desert Check-In offers People Check-Ins and Caregiver Support visits for aging adults, caregiver relief, recovery, hard seasons, and practical non-medical support.

Can you help with phones, apps, or medical alert setup?

Yes, for simple setup help. Tech & Safety Support can include basic help with phones, tablets, video calling, caregiver apps, reminders, and medical alert or safety device setup. It is not full IT support or device repair.

Do you offer reduced-fee visits or funding options?

Possibly. Some families may be able to use caregiver-support funding, respite support, dependent-care benefits, or other approved assistance for qualifying services, depending on their plan or program. Red Desert Check-In also keeps a limited number of reduced-fee or grant-funded options available for family caregivers facing financial hardship when possible.

What areas do you serve?

Red Desert Check-In serves St. George, Washington, and nearby Southern Utah communities.
