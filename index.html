About Red Desert Check-In

Local support for the questions that fall between “everything is fine” and “we need full-time care.”

I'm Jenell. I've spent nearly a decade in medical alarm response-center dispatch and supervision, working in the world of medical-alert activations, older adults, worried families and time-sensitive situations.

As a Senior Home Safety Specialist® with Aging in Place Essentials® Professional Training, I created RDC to help families notice gaps earlier, understand their options and get local support without turning every concern into medical care or caregiving.

Training & credentials: Senior Home Safety Specialist® (SHSS) · Aging in Place Essentials® Professional Training · Emergency Medical Dispatcher Certified · CPR Certified

Is RDC the right fit?

RDC is for the in-between stage.

RDC works best when an older adult is still mostly independent, but the family wants clearer information, a safer home plan, or a reliable local set of eyes. RDC is non-medical and does not provide hands-on personal care, medication administration, transportation, repairs, or emergency response. If the need is outside RDC's role, the goal is to point you toward the appropriate type of help.
