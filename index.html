Questions

Good to know before you book.

Is this medical care or home health?

No. Red Desert Check-In is non-medical. I do not provide hands-on care, bathing, medication administration, or emergency response. I look, listen, and report back so your family can make good decisions.

What do I get after a visit?

Check-ins come with a written update after the visit. Home Safety Assessments come with prioritized written findings within 3 days, so you know what to fix first.

What if my parent isn't sure about a visit?

That is common. After you book, you get my photo and a short note you can share so they know who is coming and why. You can also book a free 15-minute call first and we can talk through how to bring it up.

I live out of state. Does that work?

Yes. Many families book from a distance. You choose who receives the update, and I am your local set of eyes.

Will you try to sell me repairs or products?

No. I do not do repairs, sell remodeling, or take referral fees. If something needs outside expertise, I will tell you what kind of help to look for.

How do payment and cancellations work?

Venmo, card, or cash. First-time clients pay after the visit. You can cancel or reschedule for free up to 24 hours before. Plans are month to month.
