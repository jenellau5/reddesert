What this is

A local set of eyes for the things you're worrying about from far away.

Sometimes something feels off and you just need someone to go look. A parent who didn't answer the phone. A house sitting empty between tenants. A pet you had to leave behind for a few days. A loved one who just got taken to the hospital and family is still on the way.

Red Desert Check-In is a simple, local, in-person service for those moments. I show up, check on the person, the home, the pet, or the situation you described, and I send you a clear, honest update so you know what's actually going on.

This is not a medical service, not emergency response, and not full-time caregiving. It's a dependable local person providing presence, attention, and a reliable report when you can't be there yourself.

What I can check on

Types of check-ins

Every visit is built around what you actually need. These are the most common situations, but if yours isn't listed, ask.

  • Aging parents or loved ones

    A visit, a conversation, and a look around to make sure things seem okay. You get a clear report on how they're doing and what, if anything, needs attention.

  • Homes and property

    Walk the property, check doors, windows, water, mail, and anything else you want looked at. Useful when a home is unattended or you just want reassurance.

  • Pets

    Stop in, check on food and water, spend a little time, and let you know how your animal is doing. Not a replacement for a sitter, but a dependable set of eyes.

  • Rentals or vacant homes

    Check on a rental between tenants, a vacation home, or a property on the market. A quick walk-through and an honest update on condition.

  • Business or property checks

    For small businesses, storefronts, or properties that need a periodic local walk-by. I go, look, and report back so you know things are as they should be.

  • Custom situations

    Something that doesn't fit a category? Describe it. If it's a local check-in I can do, we'll figure out the details together.

  • Hospital support check-ins

    If someone has been taken to the hospital and family is far away or still on the way, I can stop by, sit with them, and simply be there until family arrives. This is calm presence, support, and communication — not medical care and not emergency response.

What this is not

Clear boundaries, so you know what you're getting.

Red Desert Check-In is a local in-person check-in service. It's important to me that you understand exactly what it is and what it isn't.

  • Not 911. If this is an emergency, call 911 first.
  • Not emergency response. I don't provide medical or urgent intervention.
  • Not nursing or medical care. No medication management, no hands-on care, no clinical services.
  • Not full-time caregiving. This is a check-in, not ongoing daily care.

If your need is outside what I do, I'll tell you and point you toward the right kind of help when I can.
