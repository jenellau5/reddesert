About

A local set of eyes you can trust.

I'm Jenell. I've spent nearly a decade in medical alarm response-center dispatch and supervision, working with older adults, worried families and time-sensitive situations.

I started Red Desert Check-In to help families notice gaps earlier, understand their options, and get local support without turning every concern into medical care.

  • Senior Home Safety Specialist® (SHSS)
  • Aging in Place Essentials® Professional Training
  • Emergency Medical Dispatcher certified
  • CPR certified

Is this the right fit?

Made for the in-between stage.

A good fit when

  • Your loved one is still mostly independent
  • You live far away or cannot get there often
  • You want a safer home before something happens
  • Someone just came home from the hospital

Not what I do

  • Hands-on personal care or bathing
  • Medication administration or medical care
  • Transportation, repairs, or remodeling
  • Emergency response. Call 911 for emergencies.

If the need is outside my role, I will point you toward the right kind of help.
