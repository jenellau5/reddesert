Check-in types

What can a check-in be used for?

This is one local check-in service, used in different situations. Choose the category that feels closest. If it does not fit perfectly, custom check-ins are available.

Check-In on Someone

Because you cannot always be there yourself.

  • Aging parent living alone
  • New or overwhelmed mom
  • College student away from home
  • Friend you have not heard from
  • Someone in recovery you want eyes on

Recovery Check-In

After surgery, illness, loss, or a hard season.

  • Home after surgery
  • Grief, loss, or major life shift
  • Burnout or emotional overwhelm
  • Someone saying they are fine, but you are not sure
  • Short-term support during recovery

Away From Home Check-In

When no one is around to check things.

  • Traveling or on vacation
  • Second homes and snowbird properties
  • Mail, trash, doors, lights, or obvious issues
  • Quick peace-of-mind walkthrough

Pet Check-In

Quick visits while you are away.

  • Food and water check
  • Letting them out or quick attention
  • Backup between regular pet care visits
  • Not full-time pet sitting

Property & Business Check-In

An extra set of eyes when you need verification.

  • Vacation rentals and Airbnbs
  • New build or construction walkthrough
  • Checking a home before buying
  • Verifying cleaning or maintenance
  • Business spot check or complaint follow-up

Custom Check-In

For the thing that does not fit neatly in a box.

  • Something feels off and you want it checked
  • A unique one-time request
  • A specific person, place, item, or area
  • “I just need someone local to go look”

During the visit

What a check-in may include

  • In-person check-in visit
  • Basic walkthrough and eyes on the situation
  • Conversation and connection when appropriate
  • Small help during the visit, like trash, packages, or light reset
  • Photos when useful and appropriate
  • Clear update sent to one designated contact

Clear boundaries

What this is not

  • Not emergency response
  • Not nursing care
  • Not medication management
  • Not full-time caregiving
  • Not full housekeeping
  • Not a replacement for 911

Why it matters

I do not just stop by. I pay attention.

A check-in is not just a quick knock on the door. I look for the small things people often miss: changes in the home, signs someone may be struggling, things that need to be handled, and what may need more support next.
