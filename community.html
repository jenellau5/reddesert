Community Board · Washington County, Utah

Local help for older adults and the families who love them.

A short, checked list of the places I point families to most: meals, rides, Medicare help, veterans services, dementia support, legal help, and what to do about scams. Free to use. No one pays to be listed.

In an emergency, call 911. Veterans in crisis: dial 988, then press 1.

Utah 211

A free, confidential helpline that connects you to local help with housing, food, utilities, health care and more. A good first call when you are not sure where to start.

Dial 211(888) 826-9790Website

Text your zip code to (801) 845-2211. Calls daily 8:30 a.m. to 4:45 p.m.

Rides

SunTran Paratransit

Door-to-door rides for people with a qualifying disability who can't get to a bus stop. $2 per one-way trip. Service within ¾ mile of a SunTran route. Call to apply for ADA certification.

(435) 627-4010Website
